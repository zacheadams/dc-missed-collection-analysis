Missed Collections

Where—and why—are trash and recycling pickups missed in DC?
Period: Past 180 Days (March 25, 2026 - September 21, 2026) Source: DC 311 Open Data Scope: 8 Wards • 46 ANCs • 345 SMDs • 223 DPW Routes Audience: DC Department of Public Works (SWMA)
This proof-of-concept report analyzes Department of Public Works (DPW) residential missed collections across Washington, DC. Prepared for the Solid Waste Management Administration (SWMA), route supervisors, and the Data Analytics & Research Administration, it provides spatial and operational intelligence to isolate chronic address recurrence, evaluate daily fleet workload imbalances, and support DPW's ongoing Phase 2 Route Re-Optimization. Context is grounded in testimony from the DC Council Committee on Public Works and Operations 2026 Performance Oversight Hearing and DPW's official Written Question Responses, alongside the D.C. Auditor's (ODCA) ongoing discretionary audit of residential collection timeliness.

1. Ward Performance and Address Recurrence Breakdown

Analysis of missed collections across the District's 8 Wards, comparing Trash vs. Recycling volumes and isolating repeat addresses. Under DPW's oversight definition, a "chronic miss" occurs when collection is missed 4 times within a 5-week consecutive period. Across all wards, 75.3% of impacted locations represent isolated single misses, while 24.7% (1,475 properties) face recurring collection failures requiring route supervisor attention.
